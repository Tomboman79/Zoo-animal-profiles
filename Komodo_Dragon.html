Komodo Dragon

The largest lizard in the world

The Komodo Dragon, scientific name Varanus Komodoensis, is the largest lizard in the whole world, reaching an impressive 6 to 10 ft (1.82 to 3.04 meters), they are recognizable by their brown and grey scales, rounded snouts, bowed legs, and huge, muscular legs, their habitat consists of tropical savanna forests around Indonesia.


Status: Endangered

Diet of the Komodo Dragon

The Komodo Dragon's diet is carnivorous, consisting of different animal meats and carrion, such as:

  • Boars
  • Deer
  • Smaller lizards
  • Rodents
  • Etc...

Habitat of the Komodo Dragons

The Komodo Dragons inhabit a plethora of biomes, such as arid mountains and regions, flat savannas, or rock valleys, mainly in Indonesian islands, which are the following (ranked from the most amount of Komodo Dragons to the least):

  1. Komodo
  2. Rinca
  3. Flores
  4. Gili Motang
  5. Gili Dasami

Behaviour of the Komodo Dragon

Komodo Dragons are agressive, territorial, solitary animals, you will almost never see two or more socializing.

Communication

Komodo Dragons communicate by showing agression and displays of dominance, but sometimes they also produce body odors that attract mates.

Mating

Komodo Dragons, while solitary animals, still need to mate, they do this by attracting a mate, but unlike other animals, they don't court their mates, but rather, overpower them and make them submit.

Predators of the Komodo Dragons

Komodo Dragons, being the apex predators of their areas, don't have any natural predators, not counting other Komodo Dragons
